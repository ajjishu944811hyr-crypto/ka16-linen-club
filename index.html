Our Philosophy

The Art of Pure Linen

At KA-16 LINEN CLUB, we believe that dressing well is an art form. Linen is not just a fabric; it is a lifestyle of comfort, breathability, and timeless prestige.

Located in the heart of Hiriyur, our curated fashion boutique specializes in bringing premium grade linen threads and modern bespoke menswear tailored specifically to the refined gentleman. From beach-ready casuals to high-end wedding linen suits, we customize style to embody confidence.
